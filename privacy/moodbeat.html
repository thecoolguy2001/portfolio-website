Moodbeat Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026 · Applies to the Moodbeat app for Apple Watch

The short version Moodbeat runs entirely on your Apple Watch. It does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Your heart rate and motion readings are used in the moment to pick a color, then discarded. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers.

What Moodbeat reads, and why

Moodbeat turns your body's signals into a color, like a classic mood ring. To do that, while the app is open it reads:

  • Heart rate and heart rate variability from Apple Health (HealthKit), with your permission.
  • Motion from your watch's accelerometer, to sense how active you are.

These readings are processed on your watch in real time to choose the color on screen. They are never saved by the app, never leave your device, and are never seen by the developer or anyone else.

Workout sessions

To read your live heart rate, Moodbeat starts a brief sensor session (the same mechanism workout apps use) while the app is open. This session is only used to keep the heart-rate sensor active. Moodbeat does not create, save, or change any workout, and it does not write anything to Apple Health.

Health data is never used for advertising

Data read from HealthKit is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties, data brokers, or information resellers. This is both our policy and a requirement Apple places on every app that uses HealthKit.

What Moodbeat does not do

  • No data collection of any kind — health, personal, or otherwise
  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party services or SDKs
  • No internet connection — the app has no server to talk to

What is stored on your watch

Nothing. Moodbeat keeps no history, settings, or saved data of any kind. Every reading exists only for the moment it is shown on screen.

Your controls

You can revoke Moodbeat's access to your health data at any time in the Watch app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health on your iPhone. The app will keep working with reduced accuracy, using neutral default values instead of live readings.

Children

Moodbeat is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

A note on what Moodbeat is

Moodbeat is an entertainment app. The colors and mood names it shows are a playful interpretation of sensor readings, not a measurement of your emotional or mental state. It is not a medical device and provides no medical or mental-health advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Moodbeat ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
