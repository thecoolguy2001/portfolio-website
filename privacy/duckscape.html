Duckscape AR Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Duckscape AR is an augmented reality game for iPhone and iPad where ducks fly around and hide in your real room. We built it to be fun, not to collect your data. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Data we collect

None. Duckscape has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

Camera and LiDAR

Duckscape uses your device's camera, and the LiDAR scanner where available, to show ducks in your room and to understand its floors, walls, and furniture so ducks can fly around them and hide on real surfaces. This is done by Apple's ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks on your device, in real time. The camera feed is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the 3D map of your room exists only while you play — it is not saved or uploaded.

People in view

So ducks can pass behind people who walk in front of the camera, the app uses ARKit's on-device people occlusion, which estimates where people are in each camera frame. This happens on your device only; no images, faces, or body data are stored or transmitted, and no one is identified.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Duckscape submits your best scores in each game mode to its Game Center leaderboards so you can compare scores with other players. This is handled entirely by Apple's GameKit service and is associated with your Game Center player profile, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no scores leave your device.

Data stored on your device

Your best score for each mode, and whether you have seen the first-launch introduction, are saved locally on your device so the app remembers them between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

Duckscape does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
