Reflex-o Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Reflex-o is a reflex game for Apple Watch. We built it to be fun, not to collect your data. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Data we collect

None. Reflex-o has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

Microphone

Reflex-o asks for microphone access so it can detect when you shout in response to the "Shout It!" command during gameplay. The microphone signal is analyzed on your watch, in real time, for loudness only. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere — the moment a sound is measured, it is discarded. The microphone is only active while a game is being played. You can decline microphone access and still play; the "Shout It!" command simply won't be detectable.

Motion sensors

The app reads the watch's accelerometer during gameplay to detect the "Shake It!" command. Motion data is processed on-device in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Reflex-o submits your game scores to the Game Center leaderboard so you can compare scores with other players. This is handled entirely by Apple's GameKit service and is associated with your Game Center player profile, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no scores leave your watch.

Data stored on your device

Your best score and your sound/speech settings are saved locally on your watch so the app remembers them between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

Reflex-o does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
