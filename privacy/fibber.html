Fibber - Truth Detector

Privacy Policy · Effective July 4, 2026

The short version: Fibber reads your heart rate on your Apple Watch, uses it for the duration of a test, and throws it away. Nothing is stored, nothing leaves your device, and we never see your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

What Fibber Is

Fibber is an entertainment app for Apple Watch. It compares your live heart rate against a resting baseline and displays a playful result. It is for entertainment purposes only — it does not actually detect lies and is not a medical, diagnostic, or health device of any kind.

Data We Access

With your permission, the app accesses the following through Apple HealthKit:

  • Heart rate — read live from your Apple Watch sensor during a baseline recording and a test.
  • Workout session — the app starts a temporary workout session solely to enable live heart-rate readings. The workout is discarded when the test ends and is never saved to your Health data.

How Your Data Is Used

Heart-rate readings are used only in the moment, on your watch, to calculate the difference between your baseline and your test reading. Once you finish or reset a test, the readings are gone.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not store your heart-rate data — not on your device, not anywhere else.
  • We do not transmit any data off your device. The app makes no network connections.
  • We do not collect personal information, and there are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not share or sell any data to third parties — we never have any to share.
  • We do not write anything to your Health app.

Your Choices

Heart-rate access is entirely optional and controlled by you. You can grant or revoke it at any time on your iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fibber, or on your watch under Settings → Health. Without access, the app simply can't run a test.

Children's Privacy

Fibber does not collect data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information from children is ever gathered.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Since the app collects nothing, we don't expect much to change.
