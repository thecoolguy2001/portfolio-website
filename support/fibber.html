Fibber Support

Fibber is a party-game “lie detector” for Apple Watch. It compares your heart rate while answering a question to your calm baseline.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email support@demontewalker.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

It says No Signal.
Allow heart rate access: on your iPhone, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Fibber, and turn on Heart Rate. Wear the watch snugly on your wrist.

How do I play?
Tap Start Test, stay still for the 15-second baseline, tap Start Recording while answering a question, then tap Stop Test to get the verdict.

Is it a real lie detector?
No. Fibber is a game. Heart rate changes can come from nerves, excitement, or coffee, not just fibbing. Don't use it to settle anything that actually matters.
