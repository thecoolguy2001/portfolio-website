Moodbeat Support

Moodbeat turns your Apple Watch into a living mood ring. Its color shifts with your heart rate, heart rate variability, and movement.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email support@demontewalker.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

The color isn't changing.
Make sure Moodbeat has permission to read heart rate: on your iPhone, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Moodbeat, and turn on Heart Rate. Wear the watch snugly so the sensor gets a good reading.

How do I see my readings?
Tap the screen to show or hide your heart rate, HRV, motion, and current mood.

How do I preview all the colors?
Touch and hold for about a second. Tap again to return to live mode.

Does it measure my emotions?
No. Moodbeat is just for fun. The colors are a playful take on sensor readings, not a measurement of mood or mental health, and it is not a medical device.
