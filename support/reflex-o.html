Reflex-o Support

Reflex-o is a fast reaction game for Apple Watch. The robot calls a move — Tap It, Twist It, Shake It, or Shout It — and you have a split second to do it.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email support@demontewalker.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Shout It! never works.
Reflex-o needs microphone access to hear you. On your watch, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on Reflex-o. You can also turn the mic off in the game's settings (gear icon) to remove Shout It from the game.

Can I play silently?
Yes. Tap the gear icon and turn off Voice. Commands will show as text only.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.
Scores are submitted through Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (Settings → Game Center).

Is my voice recorded?
Never. The mic only measures how loud you are, in the moment, on your watch.
