Duckscape AR Support

Duckscape AR brings ducks into your real room. In Shoot mode they fly around you and you aim with the crosshair; in Find mode a duck hides somewhere on your floor or furniture and you hunt it down.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email support@demontewalker.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Find mode is crossed out.
Find mode scans your room in 3D, which needs an iPhone or iPad with a LiDAR scanner. Shoot mode works on any device that supports AR.

No ducks are showing up.
Duckscape needs camera access. Open Settings → Duckscape and turn on Camera. Then slowly move your device around the room in good lighting so it can map the space.

I can't find the hidden duck.
Listen for its quack — the sound comes from where it's hiding. If you've been searching for 30 seconds, the eye button unlocks a hint that shows where it is.

Ducks hide in strange places or I moved furniture.
Tap the rescan button in Find mode to map the room again.

I can't hear any sounds.
Duckscape follows your device's silent switch. Turn off silent mode and turn the volume up.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.
Scores are submitted through Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center on your device (Settings → Game Center).

Play safely.
Clear some space and stay aware of your surroundings — furniture, stairs, pets, and people — while you move around.

Privacy

Duckscape collects no data. The camera and room scan are processed on your device and never saved. Read the full privacy policy.
